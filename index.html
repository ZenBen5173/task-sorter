GUIDE BOOK

Every day throws jobs at you. Some matter, some are just loud. This book is how you tell them apart.

The two questions

Before every card, ask yourself two things:

  1. Does this actually matter? Will skipping it cause a real problem?
  2. Does it have to be now? Is there a deadline today?

Your two answers point at one corner. That is the whole game.

The trap everyone falls into

Urgent and important are not the same thing. A buzzing phone feels urgent and almost never is. A big exam feels far away and is the most important thing you own.

Do Later is the corner that changes your life, and it is the one people skip, because nothing bad happens today if you do.

All four corners are live from Level 1. What makes the early levels easy is the cards - Level 1 only deals ones with an obvious answer, and the tricky ones arrive later.

How to play

  • A card appears. Drag or flick it into a corner - or tap the corner. On a laptop: Q W A S.
  • Get it right and your combo climbs, up to x8. Every card is worth more.
  • Get it wrong and the combo drops to x1. That is the real punishment.
  • Sort within 1.2 seconds for a Perfect and a big bonus.
  • The last stretch of a level is the final rush: double points.

How to pass

One thing decides it: the share you got right. Level 1 needs 60%, and it climbs to 75% by Level 4. Speed only changes your score, never whether you pass.

Every mistake waits on the end screen under What you missed, with the reason. That is the part worth reading.

Your gear

  • Weapon - more points per card, and harder hits on a boss.
  • Armour - a shield that covers wrong answers so they do not count.
  • Pet - pays extra coins, but speeds your clock up. Less time, smaller score. That is the deal.
  • Wear all three at once for +25% coins.

Boss fights

A boss sorts the same cards you do, on their own clock. Sort right to hit them, sort wrong and they hit you. Watch the bar under them - when it fills, they swing.

Why this game exists

The four corners are the Eisenhower Matrix, and it is taught in management courses to adults who pay for them. There is nothing in it a twelve year old cannot do - the only reason most people meet it at thirty is that nobody showed them at twelve.

That is the gap this is aimed at, and it is why the game is free, runs on any phone, and needs no account to play: UN Goal 4, Quality Education - a life skill, for anyone, at no cost.

If you remember one thing

Loud is not the same as important. Most of what shouts at you today belongs in Drop or Give Away - and the thing that actually matters is sitting quietly in Do Later.
