Every day throws jobs at you. Some matter, some are just loud. This book is how you tell them apart.

The two questions

Before every card, ask yourself two things:

  1. Does this actually matter? Will skipping it cause a real problem?
  2. Does it have to be now? Is there a deadline today?

Your two answers point at one corner. That is the whole game.

The four corners

Do Now Matters and due today

Pot boiling over. Homework due at 8am.

Do Later Matters, but not yet

Exam in 3 weeks. Dentist with no toothache.

Give Away Urgent, but not yours

The bin, on your brother's night.

Drop Neither

Checking who viewed your story.

All four are live from Level 1. What makes the early levels easy is the cards, not the corners - Level 1 only deals ones with an obvious answer, and the tricky ones arrive later.

The trap everyone falls into

Urgent and important are not the same thing. A buzzing phone feels urgent and almost never is. A big exam feels far away and is the most important thing you own.

Do Later is the corner that changes your life, and it is the one people skip, because nothing bad happens today if you do.

How to play

  • A card appears. Swipe it into a corner. On a laptop: Q W A S.
  • Get it right and your streak climbs, up to x8. Every card is worth more.
  • Get it wrong and the streak drops to x1. That is the real punishment.
  • Sort within 1.2 seconds for a Perfect and a big bonus.

How to pass

One thing decides it: the share you got right. Level 1 needs 60%, and it climbs to 75% by Level 4. Speed only changes your score, never whether you pass.

Get one wrong and the round does not stop to lecture you - you just hear it and see the card fly the wrong way. Every mistake is waiting on the end screen under What you missed, with the right answer and the reason, for as long as you want to sit and read it. That is the part worth reading.

Your gear

  • Weapon - more points per card. Safe, always good.
  • Armour - covers wrong answers so they do not count against you.
  • Pet - pays extra coins, but speeds your clock up. Less time, smaller score. That is the deal.
  • Wear all three at once for +25% coins.

Battle

A rival sorts the same cards you do, on their own clock. Sort right to hit them, sort wrong and they hit you. Your weapon decides how hard.

Change the background

In a level, the round button in the top right corner opens the background picker. Sakura, forest, waterfalls, or plain deep space. Pick one and it stays that way for every round after.

The clock stops while the picker is open, so changing it never costs you a second.

If you remember one thing

Loud is not the same as important. Most of what shouts at you today belongs in Drop or Give Away - and the thing that actually matters is sitting quietly in Do Later.
