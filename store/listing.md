## Full description

Task Sorter is a fast, arcade sorting game built on the **Eisenhower Matrix** -
the way managers decide what to do first, turned into a game anyone can play.

Task cards fly in. Swipe each one into the right corner:

- **Do Now** - it matters, and it is due
- **Do Later** - it matters, but not yet
- **Give Away** - it is urgent, but it is not yours
- **Drop** - neither

Sort fast to build a combo up to x8. Land a Perfect. Survive the final rush.

**Four places from your own day** - home, school, your phone, out in town -
each with real, everyday tasks, and each with its own twist: golden cards worth
triple, frozen cards you have to crack, burning cards you have to beat, and
trays that will not stay put.

**Four bosses**, each with a trick: Sticky Steve glues your cards down, Clip
Carla swaps your trays, Inbox Ivan floods your screen, and Deadline Dan sets
everything on fire.

**Come back every day.** A Daily Challenge with the same cards for everyone,
streak bonuses, rotating missions and sixteen badges to earn.

**Dress up your sorter** with 13 pets, 5 shields and 5 swords - and every piece
of gear changes how a round plays.

**Then use it for real.** My List lets you type in your own week and sort it
with the same four corners. Nobody marks it. What you get is a plan.

- Free, with no ads and no account needed
- Plays offline
- Bigger-text and calm-screen settings
- Every mistake comes with the reason, so you learn as you play

Made for UN Sustainable Development Goal 4, Quality Education: a life skill
most people only meet at work, for anyone, at no cost.
